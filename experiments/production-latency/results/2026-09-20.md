## Interpretation

For the measured successful single-operation path, Overcenter's local correctness machinery is not the latency bottleneck. The live transaction completed in 1.63-1.99 seconds, while local authority, reservation, adapter work, verification, and settlement consumed 6.5-48.0 ms per sample.

That is operationally credible for autonomous engineering actions whose underlying work is typically much slower than a few milliseconds. It does **not** establish high-throughput provider mutation: GitHub request latency and rate limits remain external ceilings.

The next performance risk is different: current project projection may refresh historical mutable realizations. This experiment deliberately isolates one fresh obligation and therefore does not measure how provider readback grows with a large history of mutable `DONE` work.
