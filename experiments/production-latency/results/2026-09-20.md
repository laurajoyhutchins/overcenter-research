## Interpretation

For the measured successful single-operation path, Overcenter's local correctness machinery is not the latency bottleneck. All three live transactions completed in 1.37-1.66 seconds, while local authority, reservation, effect-boundary bookkeeping, verification, and settlement consumed 6.8-18.6 ms.

That is operationally credible for autonomous engineering actions whose useful work generally takes far longer than a few milliseconds. This does **not** establish high-throughput provider mutation: GitHub latency and rate limits remain external ceilings.

The next performance risk is history growth. Current project projection may refresh historical mutable realizations. This experiment deliberately isolates one fresh obligation and therefore does not measure how provider readback grows with a large history of mutable `DONE` work.
