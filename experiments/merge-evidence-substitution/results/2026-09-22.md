## Falsifier

Current self-application execution identity contains both:

```text
source_sha
source_tree_sha256
```

Changing only `source_sha` therefore changes the execution-context digest even when `source_tree_sha256` is unchanged. Trusted self-application attestation content also embeds the source SHA.

An exact scratch execution of the checked-in TypeScript experiment against the repository's current `src/execution-context.ts` and `src/digest.ts` passed 4/4 tests under Node 22.16.0. It reproduced this distinction with two byte-identical source trees and different revision identities.

## Consequence

Tree equality is sufficient evidence for a **content-preserving merge relation**, not for exact-execution evidence substitution.

A safe future optimization must introduce an explicit derivation that separates revision-independent content evidence from revision-bound execution/lineage evidence. Until then, the post-merge full proof remains semantically justified.
